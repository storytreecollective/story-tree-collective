About Nathan

Rooted in music, nature, and connection.

Nathan Davila is a North County San Diego acoustic guitarist whose playing blends fingerstyle technique, melodic instrumentals, and a calm, cinematic feel.

He has played guitar since childhood, and his approach is shaped by a lifelong relationship with music, the natural world, and storytelling.

Story Tree Collective is the creative home for that work: experiences designed to help people slow down, connect, and remember where they are.
